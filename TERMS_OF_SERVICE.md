## 8. ZERO-LOG EPHEMERAL PROCESSING ARCHITECTURE
8.1. **Memory-Only Processing.** In full adherence to the principles of data minimization under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, all mobile numbers submitted for inspection are evaluated ephemerally in volatile Random Access Memory (RAM).  
8.2. **Zero Storage of Personal Identifiers.** The Platform **DOES NOT** persist, archive, database, sell, monetize, or index phone numbers submitted by users. Once the response payload is transmitted to the user's client, the number is immediately purged from execution memory.  
8.3. **No PII Association.** The Platform does not associate telephone numbers with names, residential addresses, Aadhaar numbers, Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), banking details, or demographic profiles.
